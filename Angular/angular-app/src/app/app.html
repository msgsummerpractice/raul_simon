Belgian Malinois

The Belgian Malinois is an intelligent, energetic, and highly trainable herding dog known for loyalty, agility, and versatility in both family and working roles.

Samoyed

Like Dislike The Samoyed is a friendly, intelligent, and affectionate Arctic working breed known for its fluffy white coat and signature “Sammy smile.”

Boston Bulldog

Like Dislike A Boston Bulldog is a hybrid dog breed resulting from crossing a Boston Terrier with an English Bulldog, known for its friendly, loyal, and adaptable nature.
